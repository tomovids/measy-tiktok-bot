Measy Slideshow · Last updated 8 October 2026

Measy Slideshow is a small internal tool run by Measy, the meal-planning app. Every morning it prepares a recipe slideshow made from Measy's own recipe images and sends it to Measy's own TikTok account as a draft. A person on the Measy team then opens the draft in the TikTok app, adds a sound and decides whether to post it.

Who can use it

The tool is only for Measy and the people Measy authorises. It is not offered to the public, and other TikTok users can't sign up for it or connect their accounts. By connecting a TikTok account to Measy Slideshow you confirm that you are allowed to manage that account on Measy's behalf.

TikTok

The tool uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Using it is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's developer terms. Measy Slideshow never publishes anything by itself: it only creates drafts, and posting is always done by a person in the TikTok app.

Content

The recipe cards, promotional slides and cover pictures belong to Measy. Cover pictures are created with AI image generation and are labelled as AI-generated on TikTok. The images on this website are published only so that TikTok can fetch them for the drafts; please don't copy or reuse them.

No guarantees

The tool is provided as it is, for Measy's own use. We may change it, pause it or stop running it at any time. To the extent the law allows, Measy isn't liable for any loss caused by the tool not working, by a delayed or missing draft, or by changes TikTok makes to its services.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). After that the tool can no longer send drafts to that account.
